# Brightside contract rules

Sam's rules for anything we sign. If a contract breaks one, we ask for a
change before signing, or we don't sign.

- **Term:** one year at most. Renewal only by agreement, or with at least 30
  days' notice to cancel.
- **Price:** anything longer than a year, or that renews, needs a yearly price
  review.
- **Payment:** customers pay within 30 days of the invoice (net 30).
- **Insurance:** we carry $1,000,000 general liability. Anything higher needs a
  quote from our agent before we agree to it.
- **Indemnity:** we cover claims caused by our own work or negligence. Never
  "any and all claims" or "regardless of cause".
- **Termination:** either side can end it with 30 days' written notice.
- **Scope:** anything beyond the quoted services (snow, tree work, irrigation)
  is priced separately. Nothing is "included as needed".
- **Suppliers:** we pay suppliers net 30 where we can. Flag any supplier terms
  shorter than what our customers take to pay us.
